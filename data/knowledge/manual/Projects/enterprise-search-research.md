# Research: Value of Internal Enterprise Search in the Age of Generative AI

## Background

Following Splunk's merger with Cisco, the company entered a period of organizational and technological transformation. At the same time, generative AI tools were becoming increasingly prevalent across the organization.

Within this changing landscape, the Search Team wanted to reassess the future of **Concierge**, Splunk's internal enterprise search engine, asking:

- Can traditional enterprise search remain valuable in the age of generative AI?
- How are employee search behaviors evolving?
- What role should Concierge play in the future search ecosystem?

I led a research initiative to answer these questions, understand emerging employee needs, and provide recommendations for the product's long-term strategy.

---

## Outcome

- Identified key drivers behind declining product retention and engagement
- Delivered actionable insights that informed **three new product initiatives** added to the roadmap

# Research Approach

## 1. Define Research Goals & Methods

### Research Goal

Through multiple discussions with the Product Manager, we consolidated numerous research questions into a single guiding question:

> **What are the unique and enduring values of Concierge in a generative AI landscape?**

### Research Methods

To understand employee search behavior from multiple perspectives, I conducted a mixed-method research study.

#### Quantitative

- Company-wide survey
- Large-scale behavioral analysis

#### Qualitative

- One-on-one interviews
- Deep-dive discussions

Together, these methods allowed us to:

- Identify behavioral patterns
- Understand motivations
- Discover top use cases
- Explain survey findings with qualitative context

### Participants

**Survey**

- Splunk Business Entity employees

**Interviews**

- Volunteers recruited from survey participants across targeted employee groups

# 2. Survey Findings

## Goals

The survey compared **active Concierge users** with **non-users**.

### Active Users

- Use cases
- Success rates
- Perceived value
- Expectations

### Non-Users

- Alternative search tools
- Success rates
- Search priorities

---

## Key Findings

### Adoption

Only **15%** of employees used Concierge monthly or more.

### Success Rates

- Concierge users: **30%** succeeded more than half the time.
- Alternative search users: **87%** reported success more than half the time.

### Heavy Users Still Struggled

Engineering, Sales, and Customer Experience represented the highest percentage of Concierge users, yet reported the lowest success rates.

This highlighted a gap between reliance and effectiveness.

### Top Use Cases

- Finding specific documents (47%)
- Finding IT support information (39%)

### Primary Value Drivers

- Learning unfamiliar topics (41%)
- Accessing internal company data (40%)
- Receiving relevant search results (39%)

### Employee Expectations

Employees wanted:

- Better search accuracy
- Integration with Cisco AI Assistant

### Search Behavior

Employees relied heavily on:

- Slack
- Webex

External AI assistants were used significantly less frequently than internal collaboration tools.

# 4. Key Research Insights

## Insight 1 — Concierge's Core Value

### Trusted Internal Data

Employees consistently viewed Concierge as the most reliable way to locate official internal documentation.

Unlike AI assistants, users trusted Concierge because it searched authoritative company sources.

Key themes included:

- Accurate internal documentation
- Reliable terminology
- Trusted references for customer-facing work

---

## Insight 2 — Drivers of Product Abandonment

Five primary factors contributed to declining usage.

### Missing Data Sources

Critical enterprise systems such as Slack and SharePoint were not indexed.

### Language Limitations

Support focused primarily on English.

### Inefficient Workflow

Employees searched in Concierge, then switched to Cisco AI Assistant for summarization.

### Competition from Cisco AI Assistant

Employees increasingly preferred Cisco AI Assistant for:

- Summaries
- Public knowledge
- Problem solving

### Low Product Awareness

Most users discovered Concierge only through word of mouth.

---

## Insight 3 — Fragmented Search Experience

Employees routinely combined multiple tools.

Typical workflow:

**Concierge**

↓

Find internal documentation

↓

**Cisco AI Assistant**

↓

Summarize and refine information

Rather than replacing each other, the tools complemented one another.

---

## Insight 4 — Desired Future Experience

Participants consistently described four characteristics of an ideal enterprise search experience.

### Unified Search

One search experience across:

- Slack
- Webex
- Google Drive
- SharePoint
- Other enterprise systems

---

### Context-Aware Results

Search results personalized by:

- Role
- Region
- Previous behavior

---

### Flexible Output

Employees wanted both:

**Source Documents**

- Verifiable
- Traceable
- Authoritative

**AI Summaries**

- Quick understanding
- Customizable detail
- Faster information consumption

---

### Multilingual Support

Global teams emphasized the importance of strong multilingual capabilities.

Many believed Cisco AI Assistant already performed better in this area.

---

## Insight 5 — Attitudes Toward Cisco AI Assistant

Three distinct user groups emerged.

### Adversaries

- Prioritize accuracy
- Prefer Concierge
- Concerned about AI hallucinations

---

### Light Users

- Use Cisco AI Assistant for general knowledge
- Switch to Concierge for internal information

---

### Power Users

- Default to Cisco AI Assistant
- Use search only when official internal documentation is required

# 5. Product Strategy Recommendations

Research suggested that Concierge should not compete directly with AI assistants.

Instead, it should become the trusted enterprise data layer powering generative AI experiences.

---

## Recommendation 1 — Integrate Concierge into Slack & Webex

### What

Embed Concierge directly into collaboration tools.

### Why

- Meet employees where they already work
- Increase discoverability
- Reduce context switching

### Expected Impact

- Higher adoption
- Better access to informal knowledge
- Reduced search fragmentation

---

## Recommendation 2 — Integrate with Cisco AI Assistant (MCP)

### What

Expose Concierge-indexed content to Cisco AI Assistant through MCP.

### Why

Employees increasingly expect one unified search experience.

Cisco AI Assistant lacks access to much of Splunk's internal knowledge.

### Expected Impact

- Unified enterprise search
- Multilingual AI search
- AI summaries grounded in trusted internal data

---

## Recommendation 3 — Reposition Concierge

### What

Clarify Concierge as the trusted enterprise knowledge layer with a lightweight search interface.

### Why

Employees were confused about the relationship between Concierge and Cisco AI Assistant.

### Expected Impact

- Clear product positioning
- Stronger identity
- Recognition as the enterprise source of truth

---

## Recommendation 4 — Improve Product Awareness

### What

Focus awareness campaigns on high-fit employee groups, including:

- Solution Engineers
- Customer Success
- Marketing

### Why

These users showed strong alignment with Concierge's strengths but relatively low adoption.

### Expected Impact

- Higher adoption among target audiences
- Better return on product investments

# Final Outcomes

The research helped redefine Concierge's role in the era of generative AI.

Key outcomes included:

- Identified **Customer Experience** as a new high-value user segment
- Clarified long-term product strategy amid AI disruption
- Influenced roadmap additions including:
  - Slack indexing
  - Slack and Webex integration
  - MCP integration with Cisco AI Assistant
- Validated Concierge's future as the trusted enterprise data layer powering AI experiences
- Introduced survey-based research practices to the team
