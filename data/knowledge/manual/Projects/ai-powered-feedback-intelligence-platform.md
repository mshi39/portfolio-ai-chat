# AI-Powered Feedback Intelligence Platform

*A customer feedback intelligence platform that connects insights to action.*

---

# Overview

Voice of the Customer (VOC) is a platform that bridges the gap between user experience and product development by enabling Splunk product teams to collect, analyze, and act on customer feedback.

However, customer feedback captured through VOC remained low because many Product Managers preferred gathering feedback through live customer meetings. These conversations generated richer insights but were rarely documented in a centralized system, resulting in fragmented knowledge and costly knowledge transfer.

To address this gap, the Product team expanded VOC beyond product testing to support the **scheduling, collection, analysis, and activation of customer meeting insights**.

---

## Impact

- Co-defined the scope and feature requirements for an AI-powered customer feedback intelligence platform alongside the Product Manager.
- Designed research-driven, Webex-integrated AI workflows projected to:
  - Save **20+ hours** per product testing program
  - Increase customer feedback capture by **3×**
  - Automate the collection and synthesis of customer insights

# Design Process

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Research

### The Problem

The VOC Product Manager recognized that the richest customer insights were happening outside the platform—in live conversations between Product Managers and customers.

Although VOC successfully supported product testing programs, it failed to capture insights generated during:

- Customer discovery calls
- Product feedback meetings
- Customer advisory sessions

These insights were scattered across meeting notes, recordings, presentations, and Jira tickets, making knowledge sharing expensive and inconsistent.

The vision was to integrate with **Webex** so VOC could become the place where meetings are:

- Scheduled
- Recorded
- Transcribed
- Converted into actionable product insights

---

### My Approach

To understand the current workflow, I facilitated a collaborative discovery workshop with **9 Product Managers**.

### Preparation

- Created an end-to-end journey map based on previous research
- Designed workshop activities in Miro
- Prepared facilitation materials and discussion prompts

> **Insert image:** Workshop Miro board

During the workshop, participants modified the journey map to accurately reflect their real-world workflows before presenting their changes to the group.

---

### Research Outcomes

The workshop uncovered:

- **35 total pain points**
- **5 major workflow friction areas**

#### Top Friction Points

- Legal agreements blocked teams from using VOC
- Significant back-and-forth communication to schedule meetings
- Excessive time spent reviewing raw notes
- Manual creation of executive presentations
- Manual conversion of insights into Jira work items

Working alongside the Product Manager, we translated these findings into **17 MVP requirements**.

# Phase 2 — Define the Product Direction

### The Challenge

Three architectural approaches were explored for supporting meeting-to-insight workflows.

### Option 1

Program-aware but independent workflow inside the VOC Admin Portal.

---

### Option 2

Embed meetings directly into the program creation flow.

---

### Option 3

Build a completely separate web application extending the VOC product portfolio.

> **Insert image:** Architecture comparison

---

### The Solution

Initially, the Product Manager preferred tying meetings directly to testing programs.

However, insights from user research and engineering discussions demonstrated that users wanted flexibility beyond formal testing programs.

Together, we redefined the product strategy.

The final solution treated **meeting-to-insight** as an independent workflow that could optionally connect to existing testing programs.

> **Insert image:** Home screen

---

### Impact

The new direction:

- Enables meetings with or without a testing program
- Preserves data relationships when programs exist
- Provides greater scalability for future capabilities
- Supports a much broader range of customer conversations

# Phase 3 — Validate Through Design

### The Challenge

With a compressed timeline, I chose to validate requirements through design rather than lengthy documentation.

After rapid sketching and ideation, I built an end-to-end interactive prototype.

### Research Goals

I wanted to validate:

- Were we solving the right problems?
- Which features met or missed user expectations?
- What created the greatest perceived value?
- Was the workflow intuitive?

---

### The Solution

I facilitated **5 usability testing sessions** using an interactive prototype built with **Figma Make**.

> **Insert video:** Interactive prototype

---

### Key Findings

The research fundamentally changed our product vision.

Users consistently communicated that they **did not want another meeting tool with AI summaries.**

Instead, they wanted:

> **A customer feedback intelligence platform that connects insights to action.**

Additional findings included:

- VOC should become the single source of truth for customer feedback.
- Users expected an end-to-end workflow:
  - Capture
  - Analyze
  - Prioritize
  - Execute
  - Track
- AI should assist—not replace—human judgment.
- Meeting scheduling must support real-world complexity.

# Phase 4 — Refine Around User Value

Based on research findings, I redesigned the solution around the core value drivers.

---

## Lower the Barrier to Entry

- Meetings can be scheduled without creating a VOC program.
- Meetings can optionally be linked to existing programs.
- Users can access meeting workflows at any time.

---

## Flexible Scheduling

Designed a Calendly-like scheduling experience featuring:

- Automatic participant availability detection
- One-on-one meetings
- Group sessions
- One-time meetings
- Recurring meetings

Customers can:

- Select from available times
- Decline invitations
- Suggest alternative times

---

## AI-Powered Insight Generation

Integrated with Webex to automatically:

- Import meeting transcripts
- Generate customer insights
- Cluster recurring themes

Insights include:

- Pain points
- Customer goals
- Value drivers
- Personas
- Quotes
- Suggestions

The system also quantifies recurring themes to help teams understand issue prevalence.

---

## Human-in-the-Loop AI

Recognizing that AI isn't perfect, users retain full editorial control.

They can:

- Edit insights
- Delete insights
- Add new insights
- Modify generated themes

Source transcripts remain easily accessible for validation.

Users can also customize AI-generated PowerPoint presentations by editing prompts, tone, style, and included content.

---

## Centralized Customer Intelligence

VOC now aggregates feedback from multiple channels into a single location.

Users can analyze insights from:

- Customer meetings
- Notes
- Existing feedback sources

This creates a unified customer knowledge repository.

---

## End-to-End Insight Pipeline

The redesigned workflow supports the complete lifecycle:

**Capture → Analyze → Prioritize → Execute → Track**

Capabilities include:

- Priority assignment
- AI-generated Jira ticket creation
- Jira status tracking
- Executive dashboards
- Organization-wide visibility into customer feedback
